<Game rules>
1. The Pickup action is successful only when every required cooperating agent executes Pickup for the same food in the same environment step. In a task whose rules say all agents must cooperate, the required group is the whole team.
2. The Pickup action of a food is successful only if the sum of the levels of the agents in that same simultaneous Pickup group is equal to or higher than the level of the food.
3. Every agent in the simultaneous Pickup group must be at Manhattan distance exactly 1 from that food. Being assigned to a food, moving toward it, or being close to a different food is not sufficient.
4. Success Condition: All food are picked up before {time_steps} steps.
</Game rules>
